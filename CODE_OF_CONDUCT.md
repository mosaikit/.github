# Code of conduct

Mosaikit wants to be a welcoming community for everyone, whatever their background, experience or
identity. This code of conduct applies to every repository, discussion, issue and pull request of
the [Mosaikit organization](https://github.com/mosaikit), and to every other space where someone
represents the project.

## Our standard

Mosaikit adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short, we expect everyone to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers;
- give and accept constructive feedback, criticising ideas and never people;
- assume good intentions and ask before concluding.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks and the publication of other people's
private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to **massimo.anto@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.

The maintainers decide on the consequences of a violation, from a private warning to a permanent ban
from the project's spaces, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
